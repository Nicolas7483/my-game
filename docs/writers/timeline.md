# Timeline and Chapter Plan

Placeholders for merge: [HERO], [VILLAGE], [VILLAIN], [ROMANCE], [MENTOR], [RIVAL], [KINGDOM], [MEADOW].
Calendar: years counted from the First Kindling. **BK** = Before Kindling, **AK** = After Kindling. The game opens in **AK 1000**, the Thousandth Lantern Year.

---

## 1. Deep Past

- **c. 300 BK: The Long Dusk.** The sky has no stars. Wisps of lost light drift like fireflies with nowhere to go. People live small and afraid of the dark.
- **AK 0: The First Kindling.** A choir of river folk sings the wisps into a lantern. The first star appears above the river. Magic = light that is *kept* and *shared*.
- **AK 112: The Lantern Compact.** Villages swear to share light freely. Every home keeps one lantern burning for strangers. [VILLAGE] is founded as a waystation at the river bend.
- **AK 340: The Starwell Bridge is built.** A bridge of glass and song joins [VILLAGE] to [MEADOW], where fallen starlight gathers like dew. Lanternwrights harvest it and the valley shines.
- **AK 611: The Hoarding War.** [KINGDOM] tries to bottle starlight for its capital. Stars start going dark across the sky. It ends when the Lanternwrights seal the Starwell and hide the song that opens it.
- **AK 780: The Quiet Guild.** The surviving Lanternwrights become a secret order. They teach the "Keeping Song" only in lullabies so it can never be hoarded again. Nobody in [VILLAGE] remembers why their lullaby has a strange last verse.
- **AK 950: The Dimming begins.** A star goes out each winter. Scholars blame weather. The Guild knows the seal is weakening.

## 2. Recent Past (AK 980 to 1000)

- **AK 981: The Flood Night.** The river rises. [VILLAGE] lets its lanterns go dark to save oil. A child out on the flats, the young [VILLAIN], is not found until dawn, having seen no light anywhere. The Compact failed them.
- **AK 984: The Guild's Choice.** [MENTOR], a hidden Lanternwright, sees the child can *hear* starlight. The Guild refuses to teach an "angry" pupil. [VILLAIN] is sent to [KINGDOM] as an orphan.
- **AK 990: [HERO] arrives.** A baby is found in a basket with a lantern that never needs oil. [VILLAGE] raises [HERO]. Nobody asks where the lantern came from.
- **AK 994: The Bridge Breaks.** The Starwell Bridge cracks in one night of silent thunder. [MEADOW] is cut off. Villagers blame the storm. It was [VILLAIN], now a royal light-alchemist, testing the seal.
- **AK 998: The Lantern Tax.** [KINGDOM] begins collecting "surplus light." [VILLAGE] can no longer afford to keep the stranger's lantern lit.
- **AK 1000, spring: The game begins.** The last lullaby-singer in [VILLAGE] falls ill. The river glows at night for the first time in 600 years.

## 3. Chapter Plan

### Prologue: The Broken Bridge (vertical slice)
- **Setting:** [VILLAGE] (docks, bakery, lantern shrine, mill) and [MEADOW] across the broken bridge.
- **Goal:** Rebuild the bridge (Builders-style gathering) and bring back a jar of starlight to heal the lullaby-singer.
- **Twist:** The bridge wasn't broken by a storm. [HERO]'s lantern *sings back* at the break point, answering someone's song.
- **Big choice:** A lost wisp-creature in [MEADOW] is the source of the healing light.
  - **A) Bottle it:** The singer is healed fast. [VILLAGE] trusts [HERO]. The wisp remembers being caged; the wisp clans fear [HERO] in Ch2.
  - **B) Befriend it:** The singer only half recovers, but the wisp follows [HERO] as a companion light and becomes a Ch2 guide.
- **Romance beat:** [ROMANCE] helps carry planks and teases [HERO]. You share one lantern on the walk home. First warm "maybe".

### Chapter 1: The Lantern Tax
- **Setting:** [VILLAGE] under tax collectors, the river road.
- **Goal:** Protect [VILLAGE]'s light from the royal levy.
- **Twist:** The tax collector is [RIVAL], a childhood friend of [VILLAIN], and a true believer.
- **Big choice:** Hide the stranger's lantern or pay the tax with it.
  - **Hide:** [VILLAGE] is fined and goes hungry, but the Compact survives. Travelers come in Ch3.
  - **Pay:** [VILLAGE] is safe, but the stranger's light goes to the capital and feeds [VILLAIN]'s engine.
- **Romance:** [ROMANCE] stands beside [HERO] during the confrontation. First hand-hold.

### Chapter 2: The Wisp Hollows
- **Setting:** Glowing caves under [MEADOW].
- **Goal:** Find the hidden Starwell.
- **Twist:** [MENTOR] is a Lanternwright and knew [VILLAIN] as a child. The Guild abandoned them.
- **Big choice:** Forgive [MENTOR] or expose the Guild to [VILLAGE].
  - **Forgive:** [MENTOR] teaches the Keeping Song. Healing magic in Ch4.
  - **Expose:** [VILLAGE] learns the truth and organizes a militia. More fighters in Ch5, but [MENTOR] leaves.
- **Romance:** Stuck in a dark cave, [ROMANCE] admits being afraid of the dark. [HERO] can light the way or sit together in it.

### Chapter 3: The Capital of Bottled Stars
- **Setting:** [KINGDOM]'s gleaming, sleepless capital.
- **Goal:** Infiltrate [VILLAIN]'s Lightworks.
- **Twist:** [HERO]'s lantern was made by [VILLAIN], who put the last free star in a basket and sent it downriver. [HERO] is the thing they saved.
- **Big choice:** Free the bottled stars now (riot) or steal the blueprints quietly.
  - **Free:** The city falls into darkness. Its poor turn to [HERO] as allies, and the capital is hostile in Ch5.
  - **Steal:** Stealthy and clean. [VILLAIN] never knows how close you came, and you get key intel for the Ch5 engine fight.
- **Romance:** Rooftop festival. Confession scene. Accept, defer, or gently decline (locks the romance path).

### Chapter 4: The Flats of the Flood Night
- **Setting:** The misty mudflats where [VILLAIN] was lost as a child. The dream-memory of AK 981.
- **Goal:** Retrieve the Starwell's final key from [VILLAIN]'s memory.
- **Twist:** [VILLAIN] isn't trying to hoard light. They plan to pull every star down so nobody is ever in the dark again, and the sky will die.
- **Big choice:** In the memory, light a lantern for young [VILLAIN] or leave the past untouched.
  - **Light it:** [VILLAIN] is shaken. Redemption becomes possible in Ch5.
  - **Leave it:** History holds, and [HERO] gets the key cleanly, but [VILLAIN] hardens.
- **Romance:** [ROMANCE] is hurt holding the mist back. Tending scene, and the pair's own shared lullaby is born.

### Chapter 5: The Starwell
- **Setting:** The Starwell beneath [MEADOW], plus the repaired bridge (or ruins, if the prologue went badly).
- **Goal:** Stop the Great Drawing and reseal or reopen the Starwell.
- **Twist:** The seal needs a keeper's light, meaning [HERO]'s lantern, maybe [HERO] themself.
- **Big choice:** Reseal the Starwell (safe, dim world) or open it for everyone (bright, risky, shared).
  - Feeds directly into the endings below.
- **Romance:** [ROMANCE] sings the Keeping Song with [HERO] if trust is high. Otherwise they sing it alone and carry the cost.

## 5. Endings

Tally is a hidden **Warmth** score (shared light, mercy, trust) against **Hoard** (bottling, force, secrecy).

- **Good: "Every Window Lit."** High Warmth, `WISP_FRIEND`, lantern lit in Ch4, Starwell opened. [VILLAIN] joins the song. The stars return, the Compact is renewed, and [VILLAGE] becomes the valley's festival town. [ROMANCE] and [HERO] light the stranger's lantern together.
- **Bittersweet: "The Keeper."** Mixed flags, or Starwell resealed. The world is safe but dim. [HERO] stays below as the new keeper, one lantern glowing up through the river each night. [ROMANCE] sings to it. [VILLAIN] lives, humbled, rebuilding the bridge.
- **Dark: "The Bright Kingdom."** High Hoard, wisp bottled, memory untouched, stars stolen in Ch3. [VILLAIN] completes the Great Drawing. No night ever falls, no stars remain, and [VILLAGE]'s lullaby is forgotten. Last shot: [HERO]'s lantern flickers out in an endlessly bright, silent sky.
