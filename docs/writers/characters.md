# Characters

Tone: cute, funny, heartfelt. Short lines. Every name is 4-7 letters.
Sprites come from the Ninja Adventure pack and get recolored per character.

---

## 1. Hero

**Tavi** (renameable) | `sprite: Hunter`

- Hook: Tavi washed up on the riverbank as a baby, tucked in a basket with a lantern that never goes out.
- Now grown, Tavi runs errands for the whole village and quietly wonders who lit that lantern.

**Personality through choices.** Tavi says little, but most dialogue offers three tones:

| Tone | Feel | Example reply |
|------|------|---------------|
| Kind | warm, patient | "Hey, it's okay. We'll fix it together." |
| Bold | brave, blunt | "Point me at the problem. I'll punch it." |
| Sly | playful, clever | "What's it worth to you? Kidding. Mostly." |

NPCs remember the dominant tone and comment on it ("Our bold little hero!").

## 2. Romance

### Main: Sella | `sprite: Woman`

The lanternwright's apprentice. Fixes lights, burns toast, laughs too loud at her own puns.

- **Goal:** craft a lantern bright enough to light the far meadow at night.
- **Flaw:** proud. She hates asking for help and hides her failures in a crate.

**Affinity tiers**

| Tier | Mood | Greeting |
|------|------|----------|
| 0 | Cold | "Oh. It's you. Mind the glass, please." |
| 1 | Polite | "Morning, Tavi. Need a wick trimmed or just loitering?" |
| 2 | Friendly | "Tavi! Perfect timing. Hold this. Don't lick it." |
| 3 | Fond | "I saved you the good stool. The one that doesn't wobble." |
| 4 | Close | "You're late. I was worried. I was NOT worried. Hi." |
| 5 | Love | "Every lantern I make now, I think of you. Isn't that silly?" |

**Raises affinity**
- Bringing her rare glowmoss or scrap glass (+1).
- Being honest when she asks if her lantern is good, even if it is lopsided (+1).
- Standing up to the guard or the stranger when she is scared (+1).
- Finding her crate of failed lanterns and NOT laughing (+2, once).

**Lowers affinity**
- Lying to her or flattering her work (-1).
- Mocking a villager in front of her (-1).
- Stealing from Coral's stall (-1).
- Selling her lantern to the stranger (-2, and she will not forget).

### Optional: Lucan | `sprite: Noble`

A runaway noble hiding as a fisher's helper. Terrible at fishing. Excellent at poetry about fish.

- **Goal:** prove he is useful without his family name.
- **Flaw:** runs from anything hard, including feelings.
- Affinity rises with patience and keeping his secret. It drops if Tavi reveals him or mocks his "soft hands."
- Tier 0: "Ah. A commoner. I mean, a fellow commoner. Hello." Tier 5: "I stopped running. I think it's because of you."

## 3. Villain

**Umbra** | `sprite: Inspector` (recolored ash grey and violet)

- **Wrong goal:** Umbra lost his little sister to a night with no light. He wants to gather every lantern into one Great Lamp so night never falls again. The cost: every village goes dark except his.
- **Sympathetic:** he truly believes he is saving people. He is gentle with children. He brings sweets.
- **Visual motif:** grey moths. They circle him, rest on his hat, and appear wherever a lantern has "gone missing." His own lantern burns with a violet flame.

**Chapter 1 appearance:** as a rumor first. Nettie whispers about "the Moth Man" who buys old lanterns for too much gold. Late in the chapter he arrives as a polite traveling Lantern Inspector, offering to "certify" village lights. Players who talk to him three times notice a moth on his collar that never flies off.

**Signature lines**
- "Night took everything from me. I am only returning the favor."
- "Shh. Don't be afraid. Soon you won't need to be."
- "A lantern shared is a lantern dimmed. Give it to me."

## 5. Village Cast

### Marla, innkeeper | `sprite: Villager2`
- Personality: big-hearted, feeds everyone, keeps a ledger of who owes her soup.
- Tic: calls everyone "sprout."
- "Sit down, sprout. You look like a wet noodle."
- "Soup's free. Gossip costs extra. Ask Nettie, she's in debt."

### Gil, fisher | `sprite: OldMan2`
- Personality: grumpy, secretly soft, talks to his fish.
- Tic: ends sentences with "...eh, fish?"
- "River's been quiet. Too quiet. Eh, fish?"
- "That noble boy caught a boot. Proudest I've seen him."
- **Problem:** his lucky bobber fell off the broken bridge.
  - Solve: fish it out at the riverbank. Gil gives Tavi the Old Rod and a sea shanty.
  - Worsen: keep it or sell it to Coral. Gil sulks, the fish stall closes for the chapter.

### Hobb, carpenter | `sprite: Villager3`
- Personality: can build anything, nervous about everything.
- Tic: counts on his fingers mid-sentence.
- "I can fix the bridge! One, two... I need planks. And nerve."
- "What if it falls? Three, four... What if I fall? Oh no."
- **Problem:** he needs 5 river planks and a pep talk to repair the bridge.
  - Solve (kind or bold tone): he finishes fast, the bridge is sturdy, the meadow opens.
  - Worsen (sly tone, rush him): the bridge gets built but creaks. Later a cart falls in and Gil loses his boat.

### Pim, child | `sprite: Child`
- Personality: fearless, loud, collects "treasures" (rocks).
- Tic: says "BOOM" at random.
- "I'm gonna be a hero like you! BOOM!"
- "This rock is a king. His name is Steve."
- **Problem:** Pim's night-light lantern broke, so Pim can't sleep.
  - Solve: ask Sella to fix it (+1 Sella). Pim gives Tavi the "Steve" rock, a lucky charm.
  - Worsen: tell Pim the Lantern Inspector will give a better one. Pim hands over the lantern, and the moths grow bolder next chapter.

### Old Fen, elder | `sprite: OldMan`
- Personality: wise, sleepy, remembers everything except names.
- Tic: calls Tavi a new wrong name each time.
- "Ah, young Tulip. The river gave you to us. It gives, it takes."
- "In my day, lanterns sang. Now they hum. Lazy lanterns."

### Coral, merchant | `sprite: Merchant`
- Personality: cheerful, shameless haggler, loves shiny things.
- Tic: puts a price on everything, even compliments.
- "Welcome, welcome! Browsing is free. Smiling is two coins."
- "That stranger pays triple for lanterns. Triple! My heart sings."
- **Problem:** she sold the stranger a cursed lantern and now her stall is full of moths.
  - Solve: tell her the truth and help sweep the moths out. Discounts for the rest of the game.
  - Worsen: help her sell him more. Big gold now, but the village goes dimmer and Sella drops a tier.

### Bram, bridge guard | `sprite: Knight`
- Personality: very serious about a bridge that currently goes nowhere.
- Tic: salutes before and after every line.
- "Halt! State your business. Also, the bridge is broken. Carry on."
- "I guard this spot. It is important. I think. Is it?"

### Nettie, gossip | `sprite: Villager4`
- Personality: nosy, sweet, knows everything a day early.
- Tic: whispers loudly, then says "you didn't hear it from me."
- "Psst. Sella likes you. You didn't hear it from me."
- "A moth man buys lanterns at night. You didn't hear it from me!"
